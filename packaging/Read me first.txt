Nexera Property Manager

1. Mac: double-click "Nexera Property Manager".
   When Mac says Apple could not verify the app, click Done. Do not click Move to Bin.
   Open the Apple menu, choose System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
   Scroll to Security and click Open Anyway. Confirm if it asks.
   On an older Mac, right-click the app and choose Open, then Open.
   If Mac says the app is damaged, download the latest version again from the same page.
   Windows: double-click "Nexera Property Manager". If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

2. Your browser opens. Choose a password. Your username is admin.

3. Your books are saved in Documents, in the folder "Nexera Property Manager".
   Copy that folder to a USB stick when you want a backup.

Closing the browser does not delete your books. Open this app again to continue.

Forgot the password? On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password and set a new one.
You do not need to type any commands.

The version number is on the sign-in page. New versions are on the same download page.
Quit Nexera from the sign-in page before you open a newly downloaded copy.
Your books stay in the Documents folder.
